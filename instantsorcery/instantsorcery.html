Tips for fast scans

  • Fill the outline with the card and line the name up with the bright band.
  • Hold still for a moment. A card is added after two matching reads.
  • Split cards like Fire // Ice can be held either way up.
  • Tilt foils away from lights to avoid glare on the name.
  • Older cards print the name straight onto a textured frame. Bright, even light helps most, and a reflection across the name can hide letters completely.
  • Move each card out of the outline before the next. Scanning the same card again adds another copy.
  • Your list is stored only in this browser. Export it now and then: clearing site data or removing the app deletes it.
  • If the name looks soft, the camera can't focus that close. Hold the card a little further back.
  • The page picks the rear camera that focuses closest. Use the camera menu at the top left to choose another; your choice is remembered.
